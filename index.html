The ritual

Perfume isn't sprayed. It's placed.

  1. 01

    Where

    Behind the ears, the base of the neck and the inner wrists, where warmth lifts the notes.

  2. 02

    How

    Two sprays from about 15 cm away. Don't rub, it dulls the top notes.

  3. 03

    When

    Give it an hour. The heart arrives first, then the base stays into the evening.
